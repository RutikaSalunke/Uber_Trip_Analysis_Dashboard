# 🚖 Uber Trip Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Uber trip data and generate meaningful business insights. The dashboard helps understand booking trends, customer behavior, vehicle preferences, trip distances, payment methods, and time-based demand patterns.

The solution was developed using Power BI, DAX, Power Query, and Data Modeling techniques.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall booking performance
- Identify peak booking hours and days
- Study vehicle category preferences
- Monitor revenue and trip distance metrics
- Discover high-demand pickup and dropoff locations
- Enable interactive exploration through drill-through reports

---

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- UberX is the most frequently selected vehicle category.
- Day trips contribute more bookings than night trips.
- Booking activity peaks during daytime and evening hours.
- Weekend demand is generally higher than weekday demand.
- Certain pickup locations consistently generate higher booking volumes.

## 📋 KPIs Developed

| KPI | Description |
|-------|------------|
| Total Bookings | Total number of trips |
| Total Booking Amount | Overall revenue generated |
| Average Booking Amount | Revenue per booking |
| Total Trip Distance | Distance traveled by all trips |
| Average Trip Distance | Average distance per trip |
| Average Trip Time | Average trip duration |
